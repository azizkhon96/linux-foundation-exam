	Resizing raid


	Configuring raid on cli (mdamd):
		mdadm --create --verbose /dev/md0 --level=1 --raid-devices=2 /dev/sda1 /dev/sdb2			#create raid
		or
		mdadm -Cv /dev/md0 -l1 -n2 /dev/sd[ab]1

		mdadm --detail --scan >> /etc/mdadm.conf 	#make config permenant
		or
		mdadm --detail --scan >> /etc/mdadm/mdadm.conf


		sudo update-initramfs -u

		mdadm --fail /dev/md0 /dev/sda1				#fail and remove disk
		mdadm --remove /dev/md0 /dev/sda1
		or
		mdadm /dev/md0 --fail /dev/sda1 --remove /dev/sda1

		mdadm --add /dev/md0 /dev/sdb1						#add disk

		cat /proc/mdstat	#check status
		mdadm --detail /dev/md0


		mdadm --stop /dev/md0			#stop and delete raid
		mdadm --remove /dev/md0
		mdadm --zero-superblock /dev/sda


Copying partitoon table , backup 
		MBR:
			backup and restore:
			sfdisk -d /dev/sda > partition.backup
			sfdisk /dev/sdb <  partition.backup

			copying partition to another disk:
			sfdisk -d /dev/sda | sfdisk /dev/sdb

		GPT:
			Backup and restore:
			sgdisk --backup=backup.txt/dev/sda
			sgdisk --load-backup=backup.txt /dev/sdb
			sgdisk -G /dev/sdb						#randomize GUID 

			copying partition to another disk:
			sgdisk -R /dev/sda /dev/sdb
			sgdisk -G /dev/sdb
